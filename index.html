Roberto Marfè


Chair of Finance
Collegio Carlo Alberto

Professor of Mathematical Methods in Economics, Finance and Actuarial Science
Department of Economics and Statistics (ESOMAS), University of Turin


Roberto Marfè is Chair of Finance at the Collegio Carlo Alberto, a joint venture of the University of Turin and Compagnia di San Paolo Foundation, and Full Professor of Mathematical Methods in Economics, Finance and Actuarial Science at the ESOMAS Department of the University of Turin. He serves as Director of the Master in Finance, Insurance, and Risk Management (MAFIRM). He is member of the think tank Long-Term Investors (LTI@UniTo). He also serves as an Associate Editor of Decisions in Economics and Finance. His research interests span the fields of theoretical and empirical financial economics, asset pricing, macro-finance, and mathematical finance.
